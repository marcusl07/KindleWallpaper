Leaf background behavior decision
=================================

Current product decision
------------------------

Leaf ships as one menu bar app. There is no bundled `DisplayHelper`, no embedded
login item, and no supported hidden process that continues wallpaper work after
the user quits the app.

`Launch at Login` is main-app-only. The setting maps to `SMAppService.mainApp`
and controls whether the Leaf menu bar app opens automatically when the user
logs in.

Wallpaper stability is supported while the main app is running. The app may
restore the current generated wallpaper after wake, display topology changes,
or app lifecycle events. It must not promise repair after the user explicitly
quits Leaf.

Historical helper architecture
------------------------------

The old `DisplayHelper` target, login-item embedding, helper source, helper
registration service, and helper-specific shared-defaults policy were removed
for the public beta refactor. Git history is the archive for that implementation.

Do not reintroduce a background helper during the public beta refactor. If a
future version needs wallpaper repair after explicit quit, treat it as a new
product decision with its own design, distribution, verification, and user-facing
copy rather than reviving the removed helper path by default.
